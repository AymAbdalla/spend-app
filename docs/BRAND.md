# SPEND. Brand

## Name

SPEND.

## Primary Tagline

Feel every dollar.

## Positioning

A mindful money journal for conscious spending.

## Brand Promise

Know where your money goes.
Spend with intention.

## Brand Personality

- Calm
- Modern
- Minimal
- Human
- Nonjudgmental
- Trustworthy

## Messaging Principles

- Awareness, not guilt
- Progress, not perfection
- Clarity, not complexity
- Intentionality, not restriction

## Voice

Short, supportive, direct language.

Avoid shame-based language and exaggerated
claims about behavioral outcomes.

## Visual Direction

Apple-inspired, minimal interface.

- Strong typography
- Clear spending amounts
- Generous whitespace
- Neutral surfaces
- Subtle accent colors
- Dark and light mode support

## Candidate Messaging

Feel every dollar.
Know where it goes.
Spend with intention.
Make your money visible.

## Differentiation

SPEND. intentionally requires manual entry.
That deliberate interaction is the product,
not an inconvenience to automate away.
