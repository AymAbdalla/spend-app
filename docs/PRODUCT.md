# Product Requirements

## Vision

Make digital spending feel real again.

## Problem

Digital payments reduce the physical sensation
of spending money. People can lose awareness of
individual purchases and cumulative spending.

## Hypothesis

Deliberately recording each purchase may improve
spending awareness and help users recognize
impulsive spending patterns.

This hypothesis requires user validation.

## Target Audience

People who:
- Want greater awareness of daily spending
- Struggle with unplanned purchases
- Prefer intentional financial habits
- Dislike complicated budgeting software
- Value privacy and simplicity

## Core User Journey

1. Make a purchase.
2. Open Quick Entry using the Action Button.
3. Enter amount and merchant.
4. Choose category and planned/impulse.
5. Save the transaction.
6. See updated spending totals.
7. Reflect on patterns over time.

## MVP Requirements

- Create, edit and delete transactions
- Persist transactions offline
- Daily, weekly and monthly totals
- Category summaries
- Planned versus impulse classification
- Transaction history
- Action Button quick-entry integration
- CSV export

## Non-Goals for V1

- Bank account connections
- Automatic transaction imports
- AI coaching
- Subscription billing
- Social features
- Complex budget forecasting

## Success Criteria

Users can reliably record purchases in seconds,
understand their spending totals, and review
their spending patterns without unnecessary steps.
