# SPEND. 0.1.0

Installed on the owner's iPhone 18 Pro from Xcode on 2026-11-07 and merged
into `main` the same day (commit 19c2d01). Tagged `v0.1.0` in git. Not
distributed through TestFlight or the App Store. The version number comes from
`MARKETING_VERSION` in `ios/project.yml`.

## What you can do

- Press the Action Button. SPEND. opens straight into Quick Entry with the
  keypad up and the cursor in the amount field. The same shortcut, "Log a
  purchase", also appears in the Shortcuts app, Spotlight and Siri.
- Type the amount and where you spent it, pick a category and Planned or
  Impulse, add a note if you want, and tap Save. The purchase is saved only
  when you tap Save. Cancel throws the draft away.
- On the Dashboard, see what you spent today, this week and this month, your
  last five purchases, and this month's totals by category and by Planned
  versus Impulse.
- Everything stays on the phone. There is no account, no network traffic, no
  analytics and no cloud sync. While the phone is locked, iOS keeps the
  journal files under its strongest file protection.

## How it was verified

- **Automated tests.** 27 unit and 10 UI tests, 37 of 37 passing on iPhone 17
  Pro and iPhone SE 3rd generation (iOS 26.5), zero skipped, and a Release
  simulator build that succeeded. The acceptance UI test types 12.34 and
  "Test cafe", double-taps Save, sees matching totals and one row, relaunches
  the app and sees the same single purchase.
- **Independent check.** A fresh chat with no memory of the build reran the
  suites, rebuilt a fresh clone with and without the signing file, searched
  every commit for the team ID, regenerated the project and compared it byte
  for byte, and read the evidence files. Its rows are in
  [docs/agents/checks.md](../agents/checks.md).
- **On the phone.** The owner ran the checklist in the Action Button plan on
  an iPhone 18 Pro (iOS 27.2): the shortcut appears in the Action Button
  setting, opens Quick Entry from the Shortcuts app and from the button, a
  fake $1.00 purchase saved and was still there after force-quit, lock and
  unlock, and a second button press kept a typed draft. Pressing the button on
  a locked phone asked for the passcode first. These steps are
  owner-observed: the checker inspected the ten screenshots (kept outside the
  repository under `~/aym/agents/outputs/spend/action-button/ab2/`) but could
  not reproduce them because the phone was offline.

Simulator tests prove what happens once the shortcut runs. Only the phone
proves that iOS runs it when the button is pressed.

## Known limits

- The app cannot yet show a full history, edit or delete a purchase, export
  CSV, or offer a weekly reflection. Anything saved stays until the app is
  deleted, which erases the whole journal. Use fake purchases while testing.
- There is no app icon yet, so the Action Button setting and the Shortcuts app
  show a placeholder.
- On the iPhone 18 Pro at default text size, the first recent purchase and the
  empty-state line sit behind the Add purchase bar until you scroll.
- Not tested anywhere: iOS 17 to 25, spoken VoiceOver output on a phone, Siri
  by voice, Spotlight, whether the journal files are really unreadable while
  the phone is locked, and a shortcut request that arrives while Quick Entry
  is closing.
- In the Simulator, tapping the shortcut tile in the Shortcuts app fails on
  ad-hoc-signed builds with "Couldn't find AppShortcutsProvider". The
  team-signed phone build does not have this problem.
- The phone keeps a Debug build with one fake "Test" $1.00 purchase. With a
  free Apple account the installed app stops opening after 7 days and needs a
  reinstall.
